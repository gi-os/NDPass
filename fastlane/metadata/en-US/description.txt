NDPass keeps your tickets: films, games, concerts, shows, events and trips. Photograph a paper stub, share a screenshot, PDF or confirmation email from any app, or type a ticket in. NDPass reads the title, venue, date, time, seats, price and booking reference, and files it with your other tickets for the same showing.

TONIGHT
The next showing fills the screen with its art, a countdown, your seats and directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps. A Live Activity counts down on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and reminders arrive three hours and thirty minutes before.

AT THE DOOR
NDPass shows the barcode printed on your ticket full screen, on white, at full brightness. If the photo has no readable code, it shows the photo of your stub. For tickets whose code changes every few seconds (Ticketmaster, DICE, AXS, SeatGeek), it opens the seller's app.

YOUR CALENDAR
Turn it on and every upcoming ticket gets a calendar event, unless your calendar already has one. NDPass keeps its events up to date when you change a ticket.

YOUR COLLECTION
Every stub you've kept, as a wall of tickets, on a calendar, and in stats: your streaks, your favorite venue, the team and the artist you see most, and a map of every place you've been.

READING TICKETS
Tickets are read on your iPhone, with Apple Intelligence when it's on. Nothing is sent anywhere to read them. If you'd rather, you can have Claude read them instead, using your own Anthropic API key; NDPass asks before any photo is sent.

ART
Film posters come from TMDB (free key, optional). Games are drawn in their teams' colors, with crests if you add a TheSportsDB key. Concerts get the artist's photo from Deezer.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your tickets stay on your phone.
