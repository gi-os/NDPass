NDPass keeps movie ticket stubs. Reading a ticket automatically uses the user's own Anthropic API key (Settings > Anthropic key). No key or AI consent is needed to review the app: tap + and pick any photo, choose "Not now, I'll type them" on the consent screen, and the ticket opens for manual entry. Posters use an optional TMDB key.

The app asks for consent (Guideline 5.1.2) before any photo is sent to Anthropic, and the setting can be changed in Settings. There is no account and no server.
