NDPass keeps tickets (films, games, concerts, shows, events, trips). There is no account, no server and no login. No API key is needed to review the app.

To add a ticket:
- Tap + on the first tab and choose "Type it in" to enter one by hand, or
- Tap + > "Pick a photo or screenshot" and pick a photo of any ticket. It's read on the device with Apple's Vision framework (and Apple Intelligence when available). Nothing is sent off the device to read it.
- From any app, share a screenshot, PDF, link or confirmation email to NDPass with the share sheet; the ticket is read and added from the share sheet itself.

Optional features that use the user's own keys, entered in Settings: reading tickets with Anthropic's Claude (the app asks for consent before any photo is sent, per guideline 5.1.2), film posters from TMDB, team crests from TheSportsDB. Concert artist photos come from Deezer's public API.

Calendar: off by default. Settings > Calendar > "Add tickets to my calendar" asks for calendar access and adds an event for each upcoming ticket unless one already exists.

Live Activity: for a ticket starting within 30 minutes (or scheduled ahead on iOS 26), a countdown appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
