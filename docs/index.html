NDPass

Tickets for every night out on iPhone: films, games, concerts, shows, events and trips. NDPass reads a ticket from a photo, a screenshot, a PDF or a confirmation email, keeps it with your other tickets, counts down to it and shows its barcode at the door.

Getting started

  • Tap + on the first tab to photograph a ticket, pick a screenshot, or type one in.
  • From any app, share an e-ticket, PDF, link or confirmation email to NDPass. The share sheet reads it and adds it.
  • Tickets are read on your iPhone. In Settings you can switch to Claude with your own Anthropic API key, for worn or crumpled stubs.
  • Optional keys in Settings add film posters (TMDB, free) and team crests (TheSportsDB).
  • Turn on Settings > Calendar to get a calendar event for every upcoming ticket.
