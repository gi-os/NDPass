NDPass

Movie ticket stubs on iPhone. Photograph a stub or pick a screenshot, and NDPass reads the film, theater, date, time, seat and price, keeps the collection by showtime, and shows the ticket's own barcode at the door.

Getting started

  • Open Settings in the app and paste an Anthropic API key (from console.anthropic.com) to have tickets read automatically. Without one, you type the details in.
  • A TMDB key is optional and adds posters and film search.
  • Tap + to photograph a stub or pick a screenshot of a ticket.

Questions and bug reports

Open an issue at github.com/gi-os/NDPass/issues.
