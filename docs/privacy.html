Privacy policy · updated September 2026

NDPass privacy

NDPass has no account, no server and no analytics. Your tickets, photos and keys stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your phone

Tickets, stub photos, posters and reminders are stored on your device. API keys you enter are stored in the iOS Keychain. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Ticket photos, to Anthropic. If you allow it, when you scan a stub its photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, using your own API key, so the ticket details can be read. NDPass asks before the first scan and the setting can be turned off in Settings. Anthropic's handling of API data is described at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Film titles, to TMDB. If you add a TMDB key, the film title on a ticket is sent to The Movie Database to find its poster. See themoviedb.org/privacy-policy.
  • Team names, to TheSportsDB. If you add a TheSportsDB key, the team names on a game ticket are sent to TheSportsDB to find their crests and colors. See thesportsdb.com.
  • Artist names, to Deezer and Apple. The headliner on a concert ticket is sent to Deezer's public catalog to find the artist's photo, and to Apple's iTunes Search API for album art if Deezer has none. See deezer.com and apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • Venue names, to your maps app. Tapping a venue opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with its name.

NDPass does not sell data, show ads, or track you across apps and websites.
