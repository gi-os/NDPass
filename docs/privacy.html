Privacy policy · updated October 2026

NDPass privacy

NDPass has no account, no server and no analytics. Your tickets and photos stay on your iPhone, and NDPass never sees them.

What stays on your phone

Tickets, stub photos, art, reminders and settings are stored on your device. Deleting NDPass deletes them. API keys you enter are kept in the iOS Keychain on this phone; iOS may keep Keychain items after the app is deleted, so clear the key fields in Settings first if you want them gone.

Tickets are read on your iPhone (Apple's Vision framework, and Apple Intelligence when it's on). Nothing is sent anywhere to read them unless you choose Claude, below.

What leaves your phone, and why

Only what's needed for a feature, sent straight to the service named. NDPass doesn't receive or keep any of it.

  • Ticket photos, to Anthropic, only if you set Read tickets to Claude and add your own Anthropic API key. NDPass asks first, and you can turn it off in Settings. See anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Film titles, to TMDB, if you add a TMDB key: to find the film's poster and details. See themoviedb.org/privacy-policy.
  • Team names, to TheSportsDB, if you add a TheSportsDB key: to find team crests. See thesportsdb.com.
  • Artist names, to Deezer: the headliner on a concert ticket, to find the artist's photo. See deezer.com.
  • Venue names, to Apple Maps: the Stats map looks up each venue once to place it. Tapping a venue opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with its name.
  • Links you share: when you share a link to an e-ticket, NDPass opens that page to read the ticket.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: to read the tickets you photograph or pick.
  • Notifications: reminders before your showings.
  • Calendar, only if you turn it on: to add your upcoming tickets as events, after checking your calendar so it doesn't add one you already have. Your calendar stays on your phone.

NDPass does not sell data, show ads, or track you across apps and websites.
